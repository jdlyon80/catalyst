Catalyst. Beta

A reflective practice tool for GPs and GP trainees.

A space to think through your experience in confidence, at your own pace.

How this works ▼

Catalyst holds a reflective conversation with you — asking questions, mirroring what you bring, and creating space for your own thinking to surface.

The approach is grounded in a simple observation: some of our deepest insights don't arrive through reasoning alone. They emerge when we slow down, engage with what feels uncomfortable, and sit with uncertainty long enough for something genuine to surface. Catalyst is designed to support that process.

It works primarily in a coaching register — helping you find your own ground rather than providing answers. Where you're looking for direction or external resources, it can help you think through what you need and where to find it, but that's the supporting role, not the main one.

At the end, you can generate a session summary — a brief, honest account of what you engaged with. This is yours to keep, copy to your portfolio, or bring to a supervisory conversation.

The summary also includes questions for supervision — suggested starting points based on the themes that came up, to help you take the conversation further with a human supervisor.

Privacy & feedback ▼

Your conversation stays in this browser only. Nothing is stored on any server or visible to anyone else — including the developers. When you close the tab, it's gone.

A summary is available if you choose to generate one. That summary is yours — copy it, save it, or discard it.

Tool development. This tool is only as good as the feedback it receives. Good sessions, difficult ones, moments that felt off — all of it is useful. Please share what you experienced.
